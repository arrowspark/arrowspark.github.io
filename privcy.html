Sheep Puzzle Flock - Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 25, 2026. Last updated: May 27, 2026.

TL;DR: Sheep Puzzle Flock is a fully offline single-player pixel maze game. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information about you. Everything stays on your device.

No adsNo trackingNo accountsKid-friendly

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy describes how the Sheep Puzzle Flock mobile application (the "App"), published by the developer ("we", "us", "our"), handles information when you use the App on your iPhone or iPad.

2. Information we collect

We collect no personal information. Specifically:

  • The App does not ask you to create an account, sign in, or provide an email address.
  • The App does not include any third-party analytics, advertising, attribution, A/B testing, crash-reporting, or social media SDKs.
  • The App does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, calendar, photos, motion sensors, Bluetooth, or location.
  • The App does not initiate any network requests under normal use.

3. Information stored locally on your device

To make the game work, the App stores the following on your device only, using Apple's UserDefaults API. This data is never read by us and never leaves the device:

  • Progress: levels unlocked, stars earned, best times.
  • Game balance: coins earned and total score.
  • Daily check-in: the last claim date and current streak.
  • Preferences: music / sound effects / haptic feedback toggles, and whether you have seen the tutorial.

You can erase all of this data at any time by deleting the App from your device.

4. Age and children's privacy

The App is intended for users aged 13 and older. Because we do not collect any personal information from anyone, we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you are under 13, please do not use the App. Parents and guardians may supervise younger players on a shared family device.

5. In-app purchases & advertising

The App currently contains no in-app purchases and no advertising. If this changes in a future update, we will update this Privacy Policy and clearly disclose it inside the App.

6. Third-party services

The App does not integrate any third-party services that would collect or process personal data. The only system frameworks used are first-party Apple frameworks (UIKit, AudioToolbox, WebKit) for normal app functionality.

7. International users

Because we do not collect personal information, there is no transfer of personal data across borders. The Privacy Policy applies the same way to players regardless of country or region.

8. Your rights

Under privacy laws such as the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), you have rights to access, correct, delete, or port your personal data. Since we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing to access, correct, or delete on our side. All locally stored game data is fully under your control via your device.

9. Security

Game state is stored using Apple's standard sandboxed storage. We follow Apple security guidelines for iOS applications. No data is transmitted over the network, which eliminates a category of common privacy risks.

10. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example to reflect changes in features or applicable law. The "Last updated" date at the top will always show the current version. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
